# UNSC ships of Halopedia

309 named vessels, pulled from Halopedia's MediaWiki API on 4 August 2026. Sources: every page in Category:UNSC ships, every article transcluding Template:Individual ship infobox, the five Template:Ships/UNSC navboxes, and the "ships of the line" tables on all 64 UNSC ship class articles. Hull classification symbols appear where Halopedia records one.

Ships that served the Colonial Military Administration, the Colonial Administration Authority, the Office of Naval Intelligence, and vessels later captured or defected are included, since Halopedia files them under the same category. Fighters, Pelicans, Longswords, Bumblebees and other small craft are excluded.
